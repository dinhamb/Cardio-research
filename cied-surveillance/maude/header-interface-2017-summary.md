# 2017 header-interface calibration summary

The 2017 bulk pull produced:
- 2,056 candidate device rows,
- 2,054 unique MDR keys,
- 1,958 exact-narrative event groups.

Only 11 exact groups currently carry spring-contact language, but several are highly informative because they originate around the time of the formal minute-ventilation field action.

## Pre-/peri-recall recognition

MDR 6698512 describes a DYNAGEN D153 system with non-Boston RA and RV leads. Boston Technical Services characterized the lead trends as **classic device-header spring-contact behavior**, explicitly discussed fretting with non-Boston leads, and stated that the behavior would likely worsen.

This is important because it places the engineering concept in the event record during the recall era, rather than only in later retrospective manufacturer boilerplate.

## Longitudinal event

MDRs 6737356 and 6737378 represent generator and lead reports from one system with intermittent RV pacing impedance. Later supplements document persistence over years and eventually describe likely lead-header spring-contact interaction while shock impedance remained stable.

This reinforces the need to cluster MDRs at the clinical-event/system level and preserve longitudinal supplements rather than treating every MDR row as a separate failure.
