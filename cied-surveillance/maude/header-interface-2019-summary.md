# 2019 header-interface calibration summary

The 2019 FDA MAUDE pull completed successfully:
- 3,709 candidate device rows,
- 3,707 unique MDR keys,
- 3,194 exact-narrative event groups.

After exact-text deduplication:
- 64 groups contain spring-contact language,
- 43 of those contain high-impedance language,
- 13 contain Lead Safety Switch / safety-switch language,
- 26 mention a competitor / non-Boston lead,
- 19 contain terminal-ring/fretting/particle/oxidation language.

Later supplemental/manufacturer narratives attached to some 2019 events contain the 2020 design-enhancement text; those later narratives must not be mistaken for evidence that the enhancement existed when the event originally occurred.

## Direct hardware confirmation

MDR 8719887, INOGEN EL ICD DR D143, is unusually important.

Returned-product analysis found:
- all header-port diameters within specification,
- but the **IS-1 right-atrial spring-contact component diameter was out of specification**.

Boston stated this could have produced a suboptimal connection with the lead terminal and may have contributed to the clinical observations.

This provides direct returned-device confirmation that spring-contact hardware geometry can be a real proximal-interface failure mechanism, rather than merely a diagnostic hypothesis.

## Regulatory timing

FDA approved Boston hardware modifications to the IS-1 lead-bore cavity and spring coil on **18 September 2019**. The 2019 event corpus therefore straddles the regulatory design-change decision, but exact commercial production/serial cutover remains unresolved.

Do not classify an individual 2019 generator as enhanced or pre-enhanced from marketed model name alone.

## DF-1 comparator signal

The 2019 cohort also contains DYNAGEN/INOGEN ICD configurations with IS-1/DF-1 RV connections and shock-impedance abnormalities in which ring/spring-contact interaction was considered. These should become a comparator for the emerging RV-DF4 shock-contact cohort rather than assuming the high-voltage phenotype is unique to DF4.
